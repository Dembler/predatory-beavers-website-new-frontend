# Unified Bobrik artwork

Prepared locally on 2026-11-16 from the two PNG illustrations supplied by the
site owner: «Бобр-баскетболист ВГУ с мячом.png» and
«Бобр-баскетболист с номером 100.png». Original download files are unchanged.

`bobrik-ball.png` is the canonical character. `bobrik-thumbsup.png` retains its
head, face, body, VSU uniform, emblems, lettering, number, hip hand, tail, legs,
and shoes. Only the raised arm was transplanted from the second source and its
fur color adjusted to the canonical image. The narrow newly exposed jersey
strip previously hidden behind the basketball was reconstructed from the
source armhole shape and canonical blue fabric samples. No generated lettering
or new logos were introduced.

Both full-size files are 1024 × 1536 RGBA. Main opacity was normalized from the
source's 253–254 alpha to 255, faint stray transparency was cleared, and visible
antialiased edges were preserved. The unlabelled white preview shows the two
poses at identical scale and baseline.

This is deterministic raster compositing, not a new AI generation. The poses
share the exact same visible master pixels outside the arm edit region. They
are two static poses; any web motion is a subtle transform of the complete
figure, not an articulated running or walking cycle.

The local working script and intermediate checks are in the ignored
`.data/mascot-art/composite/` directory.
